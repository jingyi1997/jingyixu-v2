Jingyi Xu

About

Hi! I am Jingyi Xu. I received my Ph.D. degree at Stony Brook University in 2024, working with Professor Dimitris Samaras. Before that, I received my B.S. degree at Nankai University in 2019. My research interets lie in the field of Computer Vision and Deep Learning, with a focus on generative modeling and representation learning.
